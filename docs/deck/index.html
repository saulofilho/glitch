01 / What it isGL!TCH

A new life for a small synth: dystopian sci-fi in four tracks.

The M-VAVE FM-1 is a pocket FM synth with 27 keys, 8 knobs, 14 buttons and a 240 × 240 screen. Its firmware can be replaced. GL!TCH turns it into a groovebox for neon-noir and darksynth: Blade Runner pads, android choirs, glitching leads, under a screen that tears with the sound.

Free and open source, installed from the browser, and you can always go back to the stock firmware.

02 / The sourcesSeven engines

Seven ways to make a sound.

VA

Cyber-Analog

Detuned saw and pulse, a sub-oscillator, a resonant lowpass.

FM

Neon-FM

Six operators, algorithms, metallic feedback: bells and glass.

SA

Cyber Lo-Fi

Four sample slots; your own WAV over USB.

VO

Android Choir

Vowel formants, and choirs made from real singers' cycles.

WH

Grid Wheel

Tonewheel organ drones, from noir chapel to grid hum.

PH

Metallic Resonator

Physical models: plucked and bowed pipes, cables, membranes.

PD

Phase

Phase distortion, as on a Casio CZ: plates and glitch leads.

Then

Tape · Grain · Delay · Space · Drive

Shared effects with a send each per track, and a master filter.

03 / Making musicSequencer · scales · generate

Write it, let it play itself, then play it live.

  • 64 steps a track, with chance, ratchets, swing and per-step sound locks.
  • 16 scales: Insen, Hirajoshi, Iwato, Cyber Dorian, Noir Pentatonic, Whole Tone… One key, one chord.
  • GENERATE: a track plays random notes of the scale by itself, in three moods: DRIFT, PULSE, STORM.
  • Perform: freeze, tape stop, reverse, echo throws, stutter, under one hand.
  • Slots and songs: four scenes, switched on the bar, chained into a song.

07 / Built and checkedEngineering

Reproducible, tested, on real hardware.

  • 302 host checks green: effects, levels, GENERATE, saves across versions, the sequencer.
  • Everything in Docker: tools/docker_refresh.sh rebuilds the browser build, the tests, the screens and the hub on any computer.
  • Attested releases: GitHub Actions builds each .fwsc from source and signs it.
  • On a real FM-1: 2.0 installed from another firmware, left, and installed back.

Found by the tests

MIXER 1 crashed the unit

A macro for the matrix rain, MX_N 20, shadowed the mixer's six tiles: a null label was drawn. It surfaced as soon as the host tests ran on Linux, and was fixed before release.
